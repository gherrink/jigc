# Compounding lessons grounding — compose accumulated lessons into future task context

**Status: parked 2026-07-02, unscheduled.** From the KB/research ideas harvest. Indexed from [VISION.md](../VISION.md) → Open questions.

## The shape

The compound-engineering loop ("each unit of work makes subsequent units easier"): learnings are recorded, and the *next* cycle's plan reads them as grounding. jigc already lives the recording half by hand (the methodology's lessons-fold-back practice; the methodology pack manages the working docs). The unbuilt, jigc-native half is the **read path**: a managed lesson/solution doctype whose *relevant* entries the CLI composes into future task context automatically — deterministic selection (by tag/area/doctype touched), prose stays the LLM's. Compounding-as-composition, not just compounding-as-recording: the accumulated record becomes live grounding through the central compose loop.

Evidence: research/07 §1 (the compound-engineering loop, `docs/solutions/` grounding, "80/20"), §3 (state passes through files, not chat memory); synthesis §3 (lessons-fold-back is jigc's maturest practice — note: that half of the evidence is self-referential; the external half is the pattern's independent ecosystem adoption).

Overlap flag: the *recording* half may be absorbed by the methodology pack's doctypes; the park-worthy novelty is the **auto-grounding composition**.

## Trigger

The methodology pack in real dogfood accumulates enough recurring lessons that manually threading them into prompts stops scaling.
