## Declared bounds carried into the build

1. **F8's error-surface interception is an unverified spike** with a stated fallback. Not a blocker;
   a builder must not silently take the fallback without saying so.
2. **F7's fence is not axis-complete** — 3 of 4 omission-instruction sites are unfenced tier-2 prose.
   Stated so the acceptance does not claim completeness it cannot have.
3. **The parity fence does not cover the prose tier.** Prose surfaces get a one-time enumerated
   census with each member listed; only value-rendered surfaces are mechanically fenced.
4. **The adapter artifact is a declared deviation from principle #5** (a detector, no delta), with
   its own trigger: when a second artifact appears, the delta discipline is owed.
5. **The manifest-hash fence protects this repo only**, not adopters, and is a build fence rather
   than a surface — the razor's edge, taken deliberately.
6. **`provision` refuses where it used to succeed.** A shipped-behaviour change, taken now because it
   is breaking after 1.0, and because the binary cannot distinguish junk from precious.
7. **One open build-shape item**: the in-task title change's verb surface (a `--task` arm on `rename`
   vs a distinct `doc` leaf). Behaviour is settled; the shape is a build decision, to be taken
   against the three-cell census rather than the adr case alone.
