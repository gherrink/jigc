## 1 · The hole: driven

An agent that wrote its rejected-options analysis where jigc's own read surfaces (`doc show --task`, `doc list --task`, M48's read-back fence) tell it its in-flight work lives loses it at exit 0, on the *ordinary success path*, on the highest-traffic verb in the product, with nothing on either channel.

## 2 · The two stock rationalizations, refuted on measurement

**(a) "M46's `--ignored` ground says narrate, not refuse."** It says the opposite once the subject is the complement.
M46 refused refusal *on a measurement*: a worktree that did its job holds build output (1 → 4 → 43 entries), so refusing fires on the ordinary success path and trains `--force` into reflex. Driven here, after `start` + `doc create` + `doc rename --task` + `task validate`, a task area holds **exactly nine files, every one jigc's** (`base.json · docs/ · docs/provenance.json · intent · renames.json · roles.json · staged-snapshot.json · workflow` + the staged `.md`s). The complement's false-fire rate on the ordinary path is **zero, measured**. M46's warrant is a fact about *worktrees*; it does not transfer to a directory only jigc writes into.

**(b) "The commit landed, so narration is the landed-boundary arm."** The landed-boundary warrant is *"the commit landed first, so the **staged set** is already in git"* (`team-ready-state.md:94`). It is **structurally unavailable here**: driven, the commit held `README.md` only; the task area holds nothing the commit takes except the promoted docs. The one justification for narrating at a landed door does not reach this subject. `finalize.md:137` authorizes the removal with no guard at all and no warrant either.

## 3 · The tells it trips

**T1 — the asymmetry M50 already adjudicated, re-minted one door over.** M50's audit LOW: `milestone discard` destroyed a sub-task's staged prose at exit 0 while `task discard` refused over identical bytes. The human took it as a **basis-has-changed reconciliation**, and the clause falsified was M46's *"its worktree refusal already carries the consent"* — false because an agent authoring through jigc writes into `.jigc/tasks/<id>/`, which the worktree probe cannot see. That is **this** argument, with the predicate one level out (`docs/*.md` instead of the worktree). Under the cheap arm, after the fix: `jigc task discard` **refuses** over `NOTES.md`; `jigc task finalize` **takes it and prints a line**. Two doors, identical bytes, opposite answers — shipped deliberately, into 1.0, three months after the human ruled that shape unacceptable. The proposer should have to say why that reconciliation stops at the abandon path.

**T2 — the machine half is a genuine one-way door, and the cheap arm cannot dodge it.** M48's standing parity fence states the rule: *"a value the human/agent text already prints, but the `--format json` envelope withholds, is a gap"*, with **additive keys pre-1.0 only** (`text_json_parity_axis.rs:1-20`; `command-output-contract.md:476-488`). Driven, the finalize envelope has **no key** for bytes destroyed or displaced. So the cheap arm has two exits and both cost the window:
  - narrate on stderr only ⇒ it mints exactly the gap that fence exists to catch. (Honest bound: `task finalize` is `Tier::Judgment`, so the suite does **not** mechanically red — the violation is of the rule, and of the census text that would have to be rewritten to bless it.)
  - carry the fact on the envelope ⇒ a **declared key on a pinned envelope**, admissible only inside the pre-pin window M52 closes.
  **The cheap arm therefore pays the one-way cost either way; what it declines to buy with it is the bytes.** A `discarded: []` key pinned at 1.0 is a contract that jigc destroys those bytes forever; a `displaced: [...]` key is a contract that it keeps them. Same window, same cost, opposite promise.
  (Conceded: the *behaviour* half is not one-way — moving bytes instead of deleting them could land post-1.0 as a patch. Only the key is.)

**T3 — the declared surface is hollow at its own law.** `team-ready-state.md:94` states *"**Silence is the one arm no door may take**"* and gives the warrant: *"the binary cannot tell `junk.txt` from `precious.txt`"*. Its scope is worktree-shaped paths — an accident of which wave wrote it (G-12/B3: leg 1 is unavailable, the wave **writes** the rule). The warrant is about bytes no object DB holds, which is the task area's exact condition. The wave is writing that rule this time; writing it as *"…unless the door is `task finalize`, where silence becomes a stderr line printed after the bytes are gone"* is writing a law with its own counterexample inside it.

## 4 · Trajectory, not generality
Two things the VISION commits to, not hypotheticals. (i) **Adapter-enforced, not sandboxed** — the bet is that jigc is the path of least resistance. `.jigc/tasks/<id>/` is where the adapter *trains* an agent to think its work lives; RC-m50's B2 went `find | xargs cat` over an abandoned task's working area thirty seconds in. Destroying bytes there punishes adapter compliance, which is the one behaviour the bet depends on. (ii) **Writes are transactional / no silent discard** — a door that takes bytes on the success path with an empty stderr and an empty envelope is the silent-discard shape, whatever the doc for the neighbouring door says.

## 5 · Is *displace* cheap? — spiked
The mechanism is **shipped twice** and is `fs::rename` within one filesystem: `relocate::displace_foreign_squatter` (`relocate.rs:282-328`, foreign squatter → `.jigc/displaced/`) and M51's `task::park_pre_image` (`task.rs:4253`, bytes → `.jigc/displaced/<name>.<nanos>`). `displaced/` is `gitignore::ENTRIES` member 7. The two task-area sinks are **one line each** (`task.rs:4948` `remove_dir_all(cleanup_dir)`; `task.rs:553`).
**Two spiked caveats, both real, neither fatal:**
- **`displaced/` has no door.** Driven: a file at `.jigc/displaced/notes.md` is destroyed by `jigc uninstall` at **exit 0**, named by nothing — and this is *declared*, in `workbench_paths`' own doc-comment (`setup.rs:3038-3046`): *"the one excluded prefix no door owns … taken by the teardown at exit 0 and named by nothing."* So the robust arm **must** carry a `displaced/` row at `uninstall`, or it relocates the hole. That row is one narration line, and the wave is already rewriting that doc-comment's home for L-2/B4.
- **"Leave the whole area" is not the answer.** Driven: after a finalize, a task-area directory left on disk makes `jigc task list` report *"1 active task(s)"* and `jigc start` orient on it as resumable. So the arm is *move the complement out*, never *skip the teardown*.
Not a new verb, not a new flag, no new user-invoked surface — the same door, a non-destroying disposition for bytes it cannot prove disposable. That is the identical shape M48→M50 landed at four other doors, so it sits inside the wave's boundary on exactly the reading that admits the cheap arm's own guards.

## 6 · The long-run price of the cheap cut
1. **Permanent, at 1.0, on the busiest verb.** Trials measure agents as reliable at push and unreliable at pull, six consecutive times; a warning on stderr *after* the removal (M50 made narration outcome-keyed) is the weakest surface in the product, and a `--format json` driver sees nothing of it at all.
2. **It ships the M50-adjudicated asymmetry as designed behaviour**, and teaches it at the door with the most traffic.
3. **The window closes on the honest version.** Post-1.0 the machine half is a versioned-extension act; the class has been found by five consecutive trials under the complete-fix lens, so "a trial will re-raise it" is a near-certainty with the cheap repair no longer available at the same price.
4. **The sink/guard asymmetry survives** (M5): `cleanup_subtask_areas` / `remove_worktrees` take the **registered set** while the guards walk **on disk**, so even the narration can name a set the sink does not take, or miss one it does.

## 7 · Robust scope — the minimal-correct cut, not the thorough one
Shared with the cheap arm at **zero marginal cost** (both need it): (a) the `TASK_AREA_FILES` registry — 12 names, 2 crates, ~10 consts promoted `pub` — plus its counted source-scan fence over `…dir.join(<literal>)`; (b) the three consenting doors refuse, `--force` the single consent; (c) L-1 fail-open, L-2 name-vs-shape, L-3 `is_file()`, the four route/ack/host-path siblings; (d) the subject rule written into `team-ready-state.md` with `finalize.md:137` taking the qualifier.
**Marginal robust delta — four items:**
1. `task finalize`'s sink moves the complement to `.jigc/displaced/<task-id>/` (relative paths preserved) before `remove_dir_all`; ~20 lines at one call site, mechanism shipped twice.
2. The move is named through the shipped outcome-keyed loss emitter **and** on the envelope as one declared key — the act the pre-pin window permits and M52 is the last wave to permit.
3. `uninstall` gains a `displaced/` row (narrate or refuse), retiring the declared "no door owns it" gap.
4. `task finalize` + `task discard` join the destroying registry with their refuse-vs-narrate discriminator. **Priced honestly:** M4 is right that this is not one line — `DESTROYING_DOORS`' vocabulary is worktree-shaped and `flow49_acceptance.rs:999-1051` reds until both matrices grow. Either generalize the table's subject from *worktree-shaped path* to *destroyed path*, or mint a second registry; decide it in those words.
Deliberately **out of scope**: any `--force` on `task finalize` (a new flag is new capability), concurrency, the sink/guard registry unification beyond naming the axis in the acceptance.

## 8 · Where cheap is right, said plainly
- The behaviour change alone is reversible post-1.0; only the declared key is one-way. I do not claim otherwise.
- Displacement without a `displaced/` door is not a fix, and the proposer is entitled to price item 3 as part of the robust arm rather than a freebie.
- If the Settle declines the envelope key, then narrate-only at finalize plus refuse at the other four doors is a *coherent* position — but it is coherent only as an explicit decision that jigc destroys un-provable bytes on its success path at 1.0, recorded with that sentence in it, not as a "visible, not prevented" bound inherited from M46, whose measured warrant does not reach this subject.

## Verdict
**robust-now.** The false-fire measurement that licensed narrate-only at worktrees is zero here; the landed-boundary warrant is structurally unavailable; the asymmetry the cheap arm ships was adjudicated unacceptable by the human at M50 one predicate away; and the machine half is a one-way door M52 closes **whichever arm is taken** — so the cheap cut spends the window and buys nothing with it. The marginal cost over the cheap arm is one sink rewrite, one declared key, one `uninstall` row, and two registry rows with a priced matrix growth.
