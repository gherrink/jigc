---
description: Plan one milestone (scope → detect gaps → settle → review → decompose) — the human-led planning loop, run from the main session, delegating recon + review to subagents.
argument-hint: <milestone-id, e.g. M3>
---

Plan milestone **$ARGUMENTS** following [milestone-planning-workflow.md](../../implementation/milestone-planning-workflow.md) — read it for the *why* of each phase; this command is its **runnable overlay** and does not restate it. Drive this from the **main session** so the human-in-the-loop phases are a conversation, not halts: delegate the autonomous recon + review to subagents, and keep **Settle** and **Decompose** inline with the human.

**1 · Scope.** Read the **$ARGUMENTS** entry in [roadmap.md](../../implementation/roadmap.md), plus [VISION.md](../../VISION.md) and [doctype-map.md](../../implementation/doctype-map.md). Restate, in your own words, what this milestone *proves*, the runnable slice it must deliver, and the worked-example flow(s) that will demonstrate it. Surface that done-picture and confirm it with the human before probing.

  **Then verify the baseline it builds on.** The done-picture is the *target*; the milestone also has a *starting point* — what the prior milestones **actually** shipped. Do **not** trust the roadmap's "shipped" prose (that optimism is what cost M3 three build halts). **Delegate** this — spawn **`capability-auditor` subagents** fanned out over the built surface (engine / CLI / pack, or by capability cluster — one assigned area each, in parallel), each **exercising** its area (run the real binary / `cargo test`, not just reading), to return a **verified capability ledger**: what is genuinely built and proven, plus the known stubbed / deferred / latent gaps (e.g. unbuilt write-verbs, latent schema defects, MVP limits). Do **not** read the codebase in the main session — keep the orchestrator's window holding the ledger, not file bodies. Present the ledger; it is the ground truth *Detect gaps* measures the done-picture against. Treat it as a **verified-at-this-sha map, not gospel** — the gap-detectors still spike the specific new shape this milestone needs.

**2 · Detect gaps.** Spawn **four `gap-detector` subagents in parallel** (one message, four Agent calls), one per dimension — **decisions**, **docs**, **doctypes**, **capabilities** — each given the milestone id, the done-picture, and its assigned dimension. Consolidate their ranked lists into one deduped, severity-ranked gap list and present it. **Treat every "this capability already exists, the milestone just reuses it" claim as blocking until it is verified against the milestone's *exact new shape*** (a repeatable section vs. a slot, a second workflow vs. a hardcoded one, a transient-source edge vs. persisted) — a claim backed only by "it's analogous to the proven path" is an `unverified-reuse` gap, not a covered one. This is where M3 lost three build halts; the gap-detectors are instructed to spike the real path rather than reason by analogy. **Three cross-cutting hunts also come back from every detector — surface them, don't let them default cheap:** (a) **cheap-vs-robust, judged against the *vision*** — where the plan or a "ready cut" leans low-effort, is it the *robust* solution or a discount that ships a fragility / forces a rework / is a regression-in-disguise? "Robust" is scoped to the **vision's committed trajectory** (its invariants + the roadmap spine), *not* this milestone's local test — the orchestrator's *"not necessary right now"* reflex is the bug. Resolve **robust-now** when the cheap cut is a **one-way door** (costly to reverse once a committed later milestone lands — a freeze, an identity model, a stored format, a contract) **or** leaves a **known hole in a goal-complete/declared surface** (a freeze, a v1); defer only a need the vision genuinely doesn't commit to (the **vision is the line, not "more"**). (b) **foreclosed-by-doc** — does a doc/decision the milestone leans on block a clearly better solution or rest on a changed basis (question it, don't obey on faith)? (c) **prior-art-reconciled** — for each thing the milestone will build, does *every* `design/` doc that touches it agree? `grep` the concept; a contradiction between locked docs is a **blocking fork**, not a free pick (don't anchor on the doc the charter/map names). All three are forks for the human, not silent picks. (M30 needed a human push for cheap-vs-robust *and* foreclosed-by-doc; **M33 needed it twice more** — the human forced the vision-robust *complete-v1* over the cheap defer-the-opens cut, and the build halted on a `prd→spec` prior-art contradiction planning had built on. The gap pass should raise all three **unprompted**.)

**3 · Settle — the human gate.** For each **blocking** gap, surface it to the human and resolve it via the [design workflow](../../implementation/design-workflow.md) — do **not** silently pick. Ask a clear question for each genuine fork (settle-now vs. defer-to-a-later-milestone; whether a gap warrants machinery at all; **the cheap-vs-robust fork with its long-run cost; whether to revise a doc/decision that forecloses a better path** — and when you do revise one, engage its *rationale*, not just its conclusion). **Record every settled decision in [DECISIONS.md](../../DECISIONS.md)** (dated, ≤1-line why), graduate any resolved `(D)` out of [decisions-pending.md](../../implementation/decisions-pending.md), and elaborate any doc / doctype schema this milestone now drives. A consciously deferred gap is logged, not forgotten.

  **Exercise the design you write here — it's a claim, not a fact** (the same discipline the baseline ledger applies, now turned on Settle's own output; both M4-audit defects trace to this step). Two musts before Review: **(a) pin the scope of every check the design introduces** — a new finding ("orphaned slot-fill blocks", "dangling ref blocks finalize") is under-specified until you state *the surface it fires against* (composed workflow? target unit? whole store?); an unscoped check is a fork the build resolves for you, often wrong. **(b) spike every acceptance flow against the real binary** — a worked-example flow that becomes the acceptance test silently encodes engine-behaviour claims ("`{{include:}}` expands in place", "composes in this order"); run the claim before writing it as the bar. Treat an acceptance-flow assumption like a reuse claim: unverified until exercised.

**4 · Review.** Spawn **one `design-reviewer` subagent** over what *Settle* produced (the new/edited DECISIONS entries, docs, doctype schemas). Bring its findings to the human and **bake the accepted ones back into the docs *before* decomposing** — an increment inherits every flaw in the design it is cut from. The human accepts or rejects each finding. (A cross-model second opinion via `/review-codex` on the settled design is fair game here too.)

**4.5 · Fill the gate-record — the forcing function (do NOT skip to Decompose).** Before cutting a single increment, **fill the planning gate-record by hand** ([methodology-docs.md](../../design/methodology-docs.md) → The planning gate-record — the table is the bar until the doctype is pack-encoded). Build it as a **per-scope-item table**: one row for **each thing this milestone will build** (each open / edge / check / feature), and a column for each gate that applies to it — at minimum **`prior-art-reconciled`** (every `design/` doc touching it agrees — `grep` it, list them), **`design-complete`** (its behaviour + finding-id/severity/scope + **acceptance test** are concrete, not a name), **`reuse-exercised`**, **`check-scope-pinned`**, **`integration-seam`** — plus the milestone-level gates **`strategic-claim-fresh`** (Scope) and **`cheap-vs-robust` / `foreclosed-by-doc` / `census` / `acceptance-spiked` / `value-flow-exercised`**. Each cell carries **evidence, or `N/A` with a one-line why** — never blank. **An empty or hand-wavy cell is a halt, not a pass**: it means that scope item is not ready to decompose (this is the by-hand form of the `required-slot-present` block the pack-encoded `planning-record` will enforce). The discipline this enforces is the one M33 violated three times by running the gates *narratively* instead of filling them: a contradiction the `prior-art-reconciled` cell would have surfaced (prd→spec), a `design-complete` cell that could not be filled (in-prose mentions), a `strategic-claim-fresh` cell read off a stale doc (the value gate). Present the filled record to the human; only then decompose.

**5 · Decompose.** Cut the milestone into ordered, **risk-first** increments straight from the reviewed done-picture **and the filled gate-record** — each with its *Deliverable* / *Grouped scope* / *Proves*, naming the design docs it builds against, in the form the [increment workflow](../../implementation/increment-workflow.md) consumes. Keep it **minimal and linear** (each increment builds on the last; no creep into a later milestone). Write the decomposition into [roadmap.md](../../implementation/roadmap.md) as a `## Milestone N — …: decomposition` section (matching the M1/M2 format), add a dated `DECISIONS.md` entry, and commit the planning artifacts.

**Done** when the blocking gaps are settled (or consciously deferred), the design has survived the independent review, and the increments are cut and ordered in the roadmap. Hand off to the build harness:

```
Workflow({ name: "milestone-build", args: { milestone: "$ARGUMENTS", base: "<current HEAD sha>" } })
```
